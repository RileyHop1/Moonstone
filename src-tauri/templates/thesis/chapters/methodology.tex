\chapter{Methodology}
\label{ch:methodology}

\section{Approach}

State the design of the study and why it suits the research questions.

\section{Data}

Where the data came from, how much of it there is, and how it was
prepared.

\section{Procedure}

Enough detail for someone else to repeat this. If a step was decided
partway through, say so — it belongs here rather than in a footnote.

\section{Limitations}

The constraints that were accepted, and what they mean for the
conclusions that can be drawn.
