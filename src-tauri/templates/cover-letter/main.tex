\begin{document}

\begin{letter}{%
    Hiring Manager \\
    Company Name \\
    Company Street \\
    Company City, Postcode
}

\opening{Dear Hiring Manager,}

Say which role you are applying for and where you saw it, in one
sentence. Then say the single strongest reason you are a fit --- the
reader decides here whether to read the rest.

Spend the middle paragraph on evidence. Pick one or two things you have
actually done that match what the role asks for, and give the outcome
rather than the responsibility. This is the paragraph a rewritten CV
bullet does not cover: explain why the work mattered.

Close by connecting yourself to this company in particular. Name
something specific about them, so the letter could not be sent to anyone
else unchanged. Say what you would want to work on and why.

Thank them for their time and say you would welcome the chance to talk.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
