\chapter{The First Chapter}
\label{ch:one}

Open with something concrete. A chapter that begins by explaining what
it is about tends to lose the reader before it arrives at the point.

\section{A Section}

Sections carry the argument; keep one idea to a section, and let the
section title say what that idea is.

\subsection{A Subsection}

Use subsections when a section genuinely splits, not to break up a long
page.

\section{Another Section}

Cross-reference by label rather than by number, so nothing goes stale
when chapters move: see Chapter~\ref{ch:two}.
